It All Started in a Parking Lot

Sounds like the start of a low-budget movie, but this one actually turned out to be worth watching.

Technically, Liam and Alyssa have known each other since first grade. The only catch? Liam preferred to keep to his small circle, while Alyssa was busy talking to just about everyone. She even went as far as hounding Liam to say hi to her on the school bus, since they grew up in the same neighborhood. But despite their early encounters, their love story didn’t quite start there.

Over the years, Liam and Alyssa went their separate ways, living different lives but somehow always ending up with the same mutual friends. Then came senior year, when fate decided to step in — in the form of four parking spots.

By some stroke of luck, their friend group ended up parking in four spots right next to each other, turning the area into the unofficial morning hangout before school. Alyssa, who was almost always a little later than the boys, quickly became the target of some good-natured teasing over her parking technique.

The boys couldn’t understand why she would pull into her spot the way she did, leaving herself to back out later, when she could simply pull through from the other side and drive straight out. After enough commentary from the peanut gallery, Alyssa finally gave in and started pulling through. Her reward? A standing ovation from the boys every single morning.

And among those boys was Liam.

The usually quiet guy took a particular interest in teasing Alyssa, much to her surprise. He’d even go as far as beating her to her car just to flip her side mirrors in — a prank he found hilarious and Alyssa, well, not so much.

But somewhere between the teasing, the parking lot commentary, and the daily back-and-forth, something changed. The two started talking more and more, and what began with childhood encounters and a few parking spot jokes turned into something neither of them could have planned.

And Then Came Us

As life went on, Liam and Alyssa found themselves doing so much more together than either of them could have imagined. Somewhere along the way, the boy who never talked and the girl who never stopped talking became best friends — and two people who chose, time and time again, to love each other through thick and thin.

From prom and graduations to milestone birthdays, family weddings, vacations, and even becoming an aunt and uncle, they’ve been lucky enough to share some pretty incredible moments together. But it hasn’t just been the big milestones that have made their story so special. It’s the everyday moments, too — the ordinary days, the little adventures, the laughs over absolutely nothing, and the simple reminder that life is just a little better when they’re together.

Every day brings another opportunity to try something new, make another memory, and experience a little more of what life has to offer side by side. And while they might not always have everything planned out (or even know exactly what comes next), there’s one thing they know for sure: as long as they have each other, they can take on just about anything.

What started with a few parking spots, a little teasing, and two very different personalities has turned into a friendship, a love, and a life together that neither would trade for anything.

And now, they’re ready for their next adventure.

So, About That Proposal…

Liam had apparently been holding on to the ring for six months before he finally managed to propose. According to Liam, the problem wasn’t figuring out if he wanted to marry Alyssa — it was finding 30 uninterrupted minutes alone with her! Between everything Alyssa always had going on and her never-ending list of things to do, Liam claims that getting her alone long enough to propose was nearly impossible. So naturally, his best option was to take her out into the middle of the ocean on a cruise ship, where she couldn’t exactly go anywhere.

As luck would have it, the final night of the trip was a formal night, and everyone was asked to take photos on a balcony before dinner. Alyssa had spent the day exploring and was feeling a little bit like a hot mess, but she went along with the photos anyway. However, she started to get the feeling that something was up.

When it was finally Liam and Alyssa’s turn for pictures, a little distraction made Alyssa even more suspicious. And then, out of the corner of her eye, she caught a glimpse of Liam getting down on one knee in the reflection of the glass.

Naturally, Alyssa immediately turned around and said, “YES!”

Liam, laughing, had to point out that he hadn’t even asked the question yet! He then went on to tell her how much he loved her, how amazing she was, and just how much she meant to him. And finally, after six months of holding on to that ring and trying to find the perfect moment, Liam was able to ask Alyssa to marry him.

Her answer? Yes — again!

And just like that, what started with childhood bus rides, questionable parking techniques, and a little bit of teasing led them to this moment. Now, after all the memories they’ve made and adventures they’ve shared, they’re ready to take on their biggest adventure yet — spending the rest of their lives together.
