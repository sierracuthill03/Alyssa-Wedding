It All Started in a Parking Lot

I know this seems like a weird place to meet, but given the circumstances of going to high school together and having assigned parking spots, the odds are better than you might think. We had mutual friends, parked next to each other, and eventually became part of each other’s everyday routine. There was some questionable parking commentary, a few broken car mirrors, and eventually Liam started walking into school with Alyssa every day. Somewhere along the way, it became less about the parking spots and more about each other.

Somewhere Along the Way

Things progressed from there in a fairly normal teenage fashion: prom, Taco Bell, movies, late-night conversations, and eventually deciding we might as well make it official. Since then, we’ve spent a lot of time doing what we like best — going places and trying things. Road trips, concerts, hockey games, Red Sox games, exploring new places, beach nights, sunsets, and picnics have all made the list. So have line dancing, bowling, fishing, and a few other hobbies that seemed like great ideas at the time. We’ve always been pretty good at trying new things. Sticking with them is another story.

And Then Came the Proposal

A few years later, Liam proposed on a cruise. Alyssa had just gotten out of the shower, wasn’t particularly interested in taking pictures, and was mostly concerned with her hair, her outfit, and the fact that she had forgotten her jewelry. Liam insisted they keep going anyway. After some suspiciously elaborate distractions and a very convincing photo session, Alyssa caught a glimpse of what was actually happening and immediately said yes before Liam even finished asking. Apparently, he had planned a whole speech. She remembers very little of it.

Now, we’re looking forward to the next part: living together, sharing rent, doing dishes, figuring out who’s cooking, and generally becoming a united front. Mostly, we’re excited to keep doing what we’ve always done — trying new things, going new places, showing up for each other, and figuring it out as we go. Only now, we get to do it as husband and wife.
