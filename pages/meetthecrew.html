Hi! I’m Sierra, Alyssa’s favorite girl cousin. Alyssa and I have been family from the start, but over the years, we’ve become a lot more than just cousins. I’ve had the honor of watching her grow from the spunky kid with pink sunglasses in her toy driveable Jeep (ask me to see the proof) into the woman she is today. Even more of a gift has been growing up beside her and getting to benefit from her wisdom and, occasionally, some tough love.

Through the years, she’s been there for me through high school, early morning drives when I’m caffeine deprived, road trips across half the country, and too many concerts to count. It’s only fitting that I get to be there for her on one of the happiest days of her life as she marries Liam. I’ve also made it a personal goal to hang out with Alyssa and Liam enough to get him comfortable enough to come out of his shell. After eight years (and maybe one more) in the making, I can confidently say I’ve been successful.

It’s been great having a backseat view of their relationship unfold and watching how perfectly they balance each other out. They have a way of making even the simplest plans an adventure, whether they’re heading somewhere new, going to a game or concert, or just spending time together. It’s easy to see how much they enjoy doing life together, and I’m so happy they’ve found that in each other.

Sierra Cuthill
