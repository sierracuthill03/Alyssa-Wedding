Questions

Are children welcome?

Kids are welcome if they were named on the invitation. If you’re unsure, just check the envelope or send us a note and we’ll confirm.

May I bring a plus-one?

Please check your invitation. If a guest is named with you, or it says plus-one, they are warmly invited. If you have a question, write us and we will sort it out together.

Where is the wedding?

Ceremony and reception are at the Roger Williams Casino, 1000 Elmwood Avenue, in Roger Williams Park, Providence. The celebration is indoors in the historic casino, with the park just outside.

What should I wear?

Attire is cocktail / summer formal — think a warm June evening in a historic ballroom. Dresses, suits, and shoes you can dance in are all welcome. Please avoid ivory and white so the maid of honor doesn’t have to “accidentally” spill red wine all over you before you are removed from the beautiful day celebrating Alyssa and Liam. Jackets are welcome but not required. The celebration is outdoor / indoor, with the park just beyond the doors.

What time should I arrive?

The ceremony begins at 3:00. Please plan to be seated a little early so you can settle in before the processional. Cocktail hour follows at 4:00, and the reception runs from 5:30 to 10:00.

Is there parking?

Yes. Parking is private for the venue. You will need to show an invitation or be on the guest list to be allowed in.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

We will have vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options. Share any notes in your RSVP and we will take care of the rest.
