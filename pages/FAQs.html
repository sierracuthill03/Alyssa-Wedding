A few things to know

Questions

Are children welcome?

We adore your little ones, and this will be an adults-only evening so that everyone — parents included — can stay out late. Thank you for understanding.

May I bring a plus-one?

Please check your invitation. If a guest is named with you, they are warmly invited. If you have a question, write us and we will sort it out together.

Where is the wedding?

Ceremony and reception are at the Roger Williams Casino, 1000 Elmwood Avenue, in Roger Williams Park, Providence. The celebration is indoors in the historic casino, with the park just outside.

What should I wear?

Attire is cocktail / summer formal — think a warm June evening in a historic ballroom. Dresses, suits, and shoes you can dance in are all welcome. Please skip white or ivory, so Alyssa can be the only one in that color. Jackets are welcome but not required. The celebration is indoors, with park views just beyond the doors, so you will not need to dress for the weather.

What time should I arrive?

The ceremony begins at 3:00. Please plan to be seated a little early so you can settle in before the processional. Cocktail hour follows at 4:00, and the reception runs from 5:00 to 10:00.

Is there parking?

Yes. Parking is available at Roger Williams Park, a short walk from the Casino. We will share any extra notes about lots or drop-off as the day gets closer.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

We will have vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options. Share any notes in your RSVP and we will take care of the rest.
