Tim has known Liam and Alyssa since elementary school. Liam lived on the same street, so Tim would randomly show up at his house and ask him to hang out. Liam was really quiet as a kid, and Tim was one of the few people he would talk to. One memory Tim still laughs about is playing Left 4 Dead at Liam’s house while his dog Spike stole a slice of pizza right off his plate.

Alyssa lived near the entrance of the neighborhood, so Tim would usually walk or bike down to hang out with her, Brian, and Grace. He learned pretty early on that Alyssa was never the one to mess with because she meant business. He has known both of them for most of his life, which makes it pretty cool to see them getting married now.
