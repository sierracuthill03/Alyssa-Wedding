FAQ

Are children welcome?

Children will be there, but primarily children who are part of the wedding party. If they aren’t on the invitation, they most likely are not invited — but feel free to reach out with any questions.

May I bring a plus-one?

We are not giving plus-ones due to a strict capacity rule for our venue. If your partner isn’t addressed on the invitation, they are most likely not invited.

Where is the wedding?

Reception is at the Casino House in Roger Williams Park, 1000 Elmwood Avenue, Providence, from 5:00 to 10:00. Ceremony details and the rest of the day’s timeline will follow with your formal invitation.

What should I wear?

Semi-Formal: knee-length, midi-length, or slip dresses for women, and tailored suits or blazers with dress pants for men. Colors should match the season avoiding jeans, sneakers, or overly casual wear. We would prefer jackets for the men; they can come off at any point. Please avoid ivory and white so the maid of honor doesn’t have to “accidentally” spill red wine all over you before you are removed from the beautiful day celebrating Alyssa and Liam. The celebration is outdoor / indoor, with the park just beyond the doors.

What time should I arrive?

Please start arriving an hour to a half hour before the start time so you can settle in. Reception is from 5:00 to 10:00. Ceremony timing will follow with your formal invitation.

Is there parking?

Yes. Parking is private for the venue. You will need to show an invitation or be on the guest list to be allowed in.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

Accommodations will be made based off of requests. Please make a note of any restrictions in your RSVP so we can provide proper accommodations.
