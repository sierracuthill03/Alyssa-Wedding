How it began

Our Story

2018

A chance introduction

They met on a rainy Thursday in a bookstore café, both reaching for the same well-loved copy of a travel memoir. Conversation spilled into evening, then into letters, then into a life they could not quite imagine without the other.

2024

The hillside question

On a late-spring walk above the river, James asked Alyssa to marry him just as the light turned gold. She said yes before he had quite finished the sentence. The ring was simple. The moment was not.

2027

A day of our own

Now they are gathering the people who shaped them — family, old friends, and the ones who became family along the way — to mark the beginning of everything that follows.

A few things to know

Questions

Are children welcome?

We adore your little ones, and this will be an adults-only evening so that everyone — parents included — can stay out late. Thank you for understanding.

May I bring a plus-one?

Please check your invitation. If a guest is named with you, they are warmly invited. If you have a question, write us and we will sort it out together.

Is the ceremony outdoors?

Yes, with a glasshouse nearby if the sky has other plans. The grounds are mostly level, though the lawn can be soft after rain.

What if I have dietary restrictions?

We will have vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free options. Share any notes in your RSVP and we will take care of the rest.
